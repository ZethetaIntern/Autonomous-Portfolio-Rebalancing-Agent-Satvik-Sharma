# ADR-003: Adoption of Tree SHAP and LIME for Multi-Audience Explainability

## Status
**Accepted**

## Date
2026-10-03

## Context
Autonomous portfolio rebalancing cannot function as a "black box" in regulated wealth management. Under SEBI algorithmic governance frameworks (Circular `SEBI/HO/MRD/DOP1/CIR/P/2024/69`), decisions must be explainable across three distinct stakeholder audiences:
1. **Retail Clients**: Require intuitive, emotionally reassuring, jargon-free narratives explaining why their money moved, how their goals are protected, and confirming zero unnecessary tax penalties (Flesch-Kincaid Grade $\le 8.0$).
2. **Wealth Advisors**: Require quantitative rigor, tracking error reduction proof, solver convergence metrics, and lot-level HIFO depletion details.
3. **SEBI Compliance Officers**: Require statutory constraint satisfaction logs, AML/suitability certifications, and cryptographic audit seals.

While generative LLMs can produce fluent text, raw LLM outputs are susceptible to numerical hallucinations and lack mathematical grounding for *why* a specific rebalancing trigger fired.

## Decision
We adopted a **Hybrid Surrogate Explainable AI (XAI) Architecture** combining **Tree SHAP**, **LIME**, and **Structured LLM Prompting**:
- **Gradient Boosted Surrogate Model**: An auxiliary Random Forest / Gradient Boosting surrogate is trained on historical portfolio rebalancing states (drift magnitude, volatility, days since last rebalance, tax friction, market regime).
- **Tree SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations)**: Calculates game-theoretic Shapley values providing local and global feature attribution ($f(x) = \phi_0 + \sum_{i=1}^M \phi_i$) for every decision.
- **LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations)**: Generates linear surrogate perturbation approximations providing interpretable local decision boundaries.
- **Counterfactual Engine**: Computes minimum-perturbation "what-if" scenarios (e.g. *What if equity drift were 2% lower? Would the rebalance still execute?*).
- **LangChain Structured Synthesis**: Injects verified SHAP values, mathematical constraints, and ground-truth numbers into Pydantic-validated JSON templates, preventing numerical hallucinations while tailoring narratives to each audience tier.

## Consequences

### Positive
- **Mathematical Grounding**: Every explanation is backed by verifiable feature attribution values, eliminating arbitrary rationalizations.
- **Interactive Visualizations**: Shapley values map directly to Plotly waterfall charts in the Streamlit Explainability Centre.
- **Audit-Proof Compliance**: The Explainability Scorecard numerically cross-checks narrative figures against actual execution ledgers.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- Requires pre-training and maintaining a surrogate model artifact.
- LIME sampling requires 50–100 perturbations per local explanation, managed asynchronously or cached for high-volume batches.

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Pure LLM Prompting without XAI**: Rejected because LLMs hallucinate numbers and cannot compute exact marginal feature contributions.
2. **Integrated Gradients / DeepLIFT**: Rejected as our rebalancing decision space is tabular and rule-constrained rather than continuous deep neural networks.
3. **Rule-Based Decision Trees Only**: Rejected because simple decision trees lack expressiveness for multi-asset nonlinear correlation and tax trade-offs.
